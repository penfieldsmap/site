Fig. 1. Key and notes

Select a segment or its label. Left and right arrow keys step along the strip. Lettering and order follow the 1950 plates. With the block-line setting, each segment has the relative length of its block line on the plate, as measured by Wright (1956). Arcs inside the strip show order relative to the labels and carry no distance. Dashed arcs mark positions reported by later studies. The head-rotation arc marks only the focus medial to the hand area. Its extent along the strip is not given by the source, and a second focus lateral to the hand area is not drawn. Solid arcs are the three function brackets of the motor figure. On a phone, drag the figure to move around it. The method codes in the records are explained under Methods.
