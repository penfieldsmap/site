Revisions and disputes

REC
Single-neuron recordings from 20 microelectrode arrays in 8 people found the whole body represented at every sampled site on the crown of the precentral gyrus, with relative strength roughly following the homunculus. [Deo et al. 2026] Applies to: all motor labels; 2023 regions
fMRI
Isometric contraction of the abdominal muscles co-activated the inter-effector regions and the cingulo-opercular network (2 participants). [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: motor figure: trunk; inter-effector regions
fMRI
Raising the eyebrows co-activated the inter-effector regions and the cingulo-opercular network (2 participants). [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: motor figure: brow; inter-effector regions

Findings

fMRI
The evidence is precision resting-state fMRI in 7 adults (172 to 1,813 minutes of resting data each). A movement task battery and a planning task were run in 2 of them, a foot, hand and tongue localiser in 3, and a larynx task in 3 adults, one of whom was not among the 7. The resting-state pattern was replicated in group-averaged data from UK Biobank (about 4,000), ABCD (3,928) and the Human Connectome Project (812). The authors caution that their schematic is not a precise map. [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: all 2023 regions
fMRI
Inter-effector cortex is thinner than effector cortex. The three regions are strongly connected to each other and to the cingulo-opercular network, the dorsolateral putamen and the centromedian nucleus of the thalamus. [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: inter-effector regions
fMRI
They show weak movement specificity. In a task with separate planning and execution phases for hand and foot movements, run in 2 participants, they were more active during planning than during execution. The effector regions were not. [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: inter-effector regions
fMRI
In the task data, abdominal movement, eyelid movement and swallowing were the preferred movements at the centres of the three inter-effector regions. The paper lists them in that order and does not state the pairing with superior, middle and inferior in a sentence. [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: inter-effector regions
REC
Lies between the foot and hand effector regions. Gordon et al. cite depth-electrode recordings (Jensen et al. 2023) as showing that this region is active during foot, hand and mouth movements. The recording study, in 13 patients, places its main shared site deep in the central sulcus at its mid-lateral part, active during hand, tongue and foot movements. Its authors say only that the superior region of Gordon et al. may overlie a secondary site, which they saw in five patients. [Gordon et al. 2023; Jensen et al. 2023] Applies to: superior inter-effector region
DES
The authors note that Penfield’s stimulation elicited movement from about half of sites, mostly of the foot, hand and mouth, and that sites giving no movement were historically not recorded. [Gordon et al. 2023] Applies to: all 2023 regions
